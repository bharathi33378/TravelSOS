# 🚨 Travel SOS

#SIH Hackothon Project 

## 📌 Project Overview

**Travel SOS** is an AI-powered travel safety and emergency response system designed to enhance traveler security through intelligent software, real-time communication, location-based services, and connected hardware components.

The system aims to provide rapid emergency assistance during critical situations by enabling users to trigger an SOS alert, share their location, and communicate important information with designated emergency contacts or relevant support services.

By integrating **Artificial Intelligence, IoT, GPS technology, and modern software systems**, Travel SOS provides a technology-driven approach to improving travel safety and emergency response.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Travelers may face unexpected emergencies, including accidents, unsafe situations, medical emergencies, or situations where immediate assistance is required. In such situations, delays in sharing location information or contacting emergency services can increase the risk.

Travel SOS aims to address this challenge by providing a smart and accessible emergency response solution.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Travel SOS combines software intelligence and hardware-based emergency capabilities to create an integrated safety system.

Users can trigger an emergency alert through the application or a connected SOS device. The system can collect relevant information, including location and emergency status, process the data, and transmit alerts to designated contacts or emergency support systems.

---

# 🔄 System Workflow

1. The user encounters an emergency or unsafe situation.
2. The user activates the SOS alert through the application or hardware device.
3. The system collects relevant information, including the user's location.
4. The emergency information is transmitted to the Travel SOS platform.
5. The software processes the emergency request.
6. AI-based systems can provide intelligent assistance or safety recommendations.
7. Emergency alerts and location details are shared with designated contacts or relevant support services.
